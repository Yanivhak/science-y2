## מה כתוב על הגרף עצמו
- „For millennia, atmospheric carbon dioxide had never been above this line" — קו מקווקו ב-300 ppm.
- חיצים: 1911 (כ-300) · 1958 (כ-350) · Today (כ-420).
- כל ה-800,000 שנה בקידוחים: בערך בין 170 ל-300 ppm.

## ציטוטים מהדף (באנגלית, כפי שהודבקו)
- „While Earth's climate has changed throughout its history, the current warming is happening at a rate not seen in the past 10,000 years."
- „Just in the last 800,000 years, there have been eight cycles of ice ages and warmer periods … Most of these climate changes are attributed to very small variations in Earth's orbit that change the amount of solar energy our planet receives."
- „Scientists demonstrated the heat-trapping nature of carbon dioxide and other gases in the mid-19th century."
- „Ice cores … show that Earth's climate responds to changes in greenhouse gas levels."
- „current warming is occurring roughly 10 times faster than the average rate of warming after an ice age. Carbon dioxide from human activities is increasing about 250 times faster than it did from natural sources after the last Ice Age."
